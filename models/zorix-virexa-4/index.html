Overview

Familiar on the surface. Much more capable underneath.

A Zorix team member described Virexa 4 as looking broadly similar to Virexa 3.5 in overall direction, while the actual capability improvement has turned out to be significantly larger than expected.

Status

In preparation

Virexa 4 has not been officially released yet.

Direction

Familiar

The overall product direction remains recognizable to people coming from Virexa 3.5.

Capability

Higher

Early internal use is showing a much larger capability improvement than the team member initially expected.

Release

May take longer

The release may arrive a little later than previously expected. Final timing has not been announced.

Compared with 3.5

Not a redesign for the sake of a version number.

The current message around Virexa 4 is not that everything has been visually or structurally reinvented. The important difference is the capability improvement seen during testing.
