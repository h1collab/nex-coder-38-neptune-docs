Day One: Resets for Everyone

The first day of our three-day Zorix Code update window is complete.
Day 1 update

Yesterday, we said that for three days we would prioritize the feedback the community was asking for most.

The clearest signal was not subtle.

Resets.

Again and again, the request we saw most often was for resets.

So that is what we are doing.

We are giving a reset to every user.

This is the first completed update in the three-day feedback window.

The point of this process is to make the feedback loop real: listen to what people are asking for, identify what comes up most often, and act on it quickly when we can.

Day one is complete.

We still have two days left in this focused update period, and we will keep watching which requests come through most strongly next.

— A member of the Zorix team
