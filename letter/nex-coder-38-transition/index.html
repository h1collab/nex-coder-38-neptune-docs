From Zorix · October 2, 2026

The Nex Coder 3.8 Transition

The 3.8 series is retired, but a short transition window remains before the final shutdown.
Letter

Today, Nex Coder 4 Plutos begins a new chapter for Zorix.

At the same time, we are completing the transition away from the Nex Coder 3.8 series.

Nex Coder 3.8 is retired.

Because this transition is still in progress, some users may temporarily continue to see or use Neptune and other models from the 3.8 series.

This temporary access does not mean the 3.8 series has returned to general availability.

After October 3, the 3.8 series will be fully shut down.

After October 3, 2026, Neptune and the remaining Nex Coder 3.8 models will no longer be available.

We know that many people have spent time building, coding, testing, and experimenting with these models. The 3.8 series helped shape what comes next, and we are grateful to everyone who used it and pushed it further.

Plutos is now available in Zorix Chat for Sovreign and Apex users, and access will expand gradually through staged gray testing.

This is a transition, not just an ending. The work continues, and the next generation is already here.

— Zorix
