Preparing Text Watermarking

We are preparing a text watermarking method for Zorix-generated text.
Letter

As AI-generated text becomes more common, we think it is important to keep improving the ways generated content can be understood and identified.

We are now preparing to add a text watermarking method to Zorix.

The goal is to introduce a signal into generated text without changing the way people naturally read and use it.

This work is still in preparation. The exact method, rollout scope, detection process, and product surfaces that will use it are not being finalized publicly today.

We are taking time to evaluate how the approach behaves across different kinds of text, languages, editing patterns, and real-world use before treating it as a finished system.

Text watermarking should not get in the way of writing quality. It should not become a visible distraction to people using Zorix to write, code, translate, learn, or build.

At the same time, any watermarking method only matters if it is useful in practice. That means reliability, resilience, and clear limitations matter as much as the basic idea itself.

This is an additional signal, not a substitute for judgment, provenance, or responsible use.

We will share more when the method is ready for broader testing and when we have more concrete details about how it will work across Zorix products.

For now, the important update is simple: text watermarking is something we are actively preparing to introduce.

— Zorix
