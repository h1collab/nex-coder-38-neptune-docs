From a member of the Zorix team · October 9, 2026

Virexa 4 Went Beyond What I Expected

It may look familiar next to Virexa 3.5, but the capability jump is larger than I expected.
Letter

I have been spending time with Virexa 4, and it has gone beyond what I expected.

The surprising part is that, at first glance, it does not feel radically different from Virexa 3.5.

The overall product direction is still recognizable. Someone moving from 3.5 to 4 may not immediately feel like they are looking at something completely different.

But once you start using it more seriously, the capability improvement becomes much clearer.

Virexa 4 is significantly more capable than I expected it to be.

That gap matters more to me than making the model look different for the sake of a new version number.

We are still testing it, and that is also why the release may take a little longer than originally expected.

I would rather give the team a little more time than rush the release simply because we previously expected Virexa 4 in October.

This is not a cancellation, and it is not a sign that the model is moving backward. If anything, the reason I am comfortable waiting a little longer is that what we are seeing is more interesting than I expected.

Virexa 4 may arrive a little later — but the capability improvement is real.

We will share the final timing when we are ready.

— A member of the Zorix team
October 9, 2026 · This is a personal note from a Zorix team member. Virexa 4 remains in preparation and its final release timing has not been announced.
