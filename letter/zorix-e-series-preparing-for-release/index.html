Zorix e Series Is Preparing for Release

A new experimental Zorix model series, built on the Zorix-developed EOP architecture, is moving toward release.
Official notice

We are preparing to release a new model family: the Zorix e series.

The Zorix e series is preparing for release.

This is an early announcement. The series is still moving through preparation before broader availability.

The name e series comes from two ideas. The series uses EOP, a Zorix-developed architecture, and the letter e represents virtual.

e1 is the first model in the series.

The initial purpose of e1 is experimental: it is designed to test and explore the new EOP architecture rather than serve as a general replacement for existing Zorix models.

We do not recommend e1 for coding. Coding workloads should continue to use models designed for that purpose, such as the Nex Coder family.

We recommend treating e1 as an experimental model for testing the new architecture, observing its behavior, and learning where the e series should go next.

We are not publishing access tiers, pricing, API availability, or detailed technical specifications in this letter.

Those details will be shared when they are ready.

EOP is the architectural experiment. e1 is the first step.

As release gets closer, we will share more about how e1 will be made available and how the e series fits alongside the rest of Zorix.

— Zorix
