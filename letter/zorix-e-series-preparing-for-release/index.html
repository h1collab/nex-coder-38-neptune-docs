Zorix e Series Is Preparing for Release

A new Zorix model series is moving toward release.
Official notice

We are preparing to release a new model family: the Zorix e series.

The Zorix e series is preparing for release.

This is an early announcement. The series is still moving through preparation before broader availability.

We are not publishing the final model lineup, individual variants, access tiers, pricing, API availability, or detailed technical specifications in this letter.

Those details will be shared when they are ready.

What we can confirm today is that the Zorix e series is real, it is in preparation, and it represents another branch of the Zorix model family.

A new series is coming.

As release gets closer, we will share more about what the e series is designed for, how it will be made available, and where it fits alongside the rest of Zorix.

— Zorix
