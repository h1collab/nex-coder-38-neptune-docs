Zorix Ascend 27P Is About to Begin

Ascend 27P begins on October 13, 2026. A large wave of releases and announcements will follow.
Official notice

We are preparing to begin Zorix Ascend 27P.

Ascend 27P begins October 13, 2026.

Ascend is Zorix's annual release conference — the place where we present major product, platform, model, and ecosystem updates across the year.

The Ascend cycle is organized around three phases:

A marks the opening phase of the annual cycle. B covers the following six-month phase. P represents the final two months of the year.

That means 27P is the final phase of the Ascend 27 cycle.

Ascend 27P will begin on October 13, 2026.

Once it begins, we plan to release and announce a lot across Zorix. Ascend is not centered on one model or one product; it is an annual release conference covering multiple parts of the Zorix ecosystem.

October 13 starts a much larger release wave.

We are still keeping most of the announcement list private until the event begins.

But we can leave one preview:

Nex…

It is not Nex Coder, and it is not a coding model. It is a new Nex product.

We will reveal what that means during Ascend 27P.

The final phase of Ascend 27 begins October 13.
— Zorix
