A Letter from Our CEO

Nine months since Zorix was founded — a moment to look back at what has been built, and forward to what comes next.
Letter

Dear everyone,

I'm writing this letter with a great deal of pride and gratitude.

Today, we celebrate a special milestone — Zorix has officially reached 9 months since its founding. Nine months ago, we started with a vision, a small team, and a lot of determination. Today, thanks to each and every one of you, that vision has grown into something real.

Over these past 9 months, we have released many models — each one a step forward, each one a reflection of your hard work, creativity, and dedication. None of this would have been possible without you.

But we're not stopping here.

I want to share a little hint about what's coming next. Virexa 4 is not far away. And that's not all — Nex Coder 3.9 and Alara are also on the horizon. Stay tuned. The best is yet to come.

Thank you for being part of this journey. Here's to the next 9 months — and beyond.

With appreciation,

CEO, Zorix
