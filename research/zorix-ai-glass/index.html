What we are doing

Studying a new way to access Zorix AI.

Zorix has started research around Zorix AI Glass. The project is still at an early stage and should be treated as research, not as a finished product announcement.

Right now, the goal is to understand what an AI experience in a glasses form factor should be, what interactions would actually be useful, and what technical limits would matter before turning the idea into a product.

Status

Early research

The project is being explored internally. No launch commitment has been made.

Product

Zorix AI Glass

Availability

Not announced

No public testing program, release window, price, or supported market has been announced.

We are intentionally not publishing hardware specifications or promising features yet. The design, capabilities, software, supported models, battery targets, release timing, pricing, and availability can all change while the project is being researched.
Research questions

Useful first. Hardware second.

Before defining a final device, we are looking at the interaction itself: when an AI system on glasses is genuinely more useful than a phone or computer, how quickly a user should be able to reach it, and how the experience can remain simple enough to use naturally.

This work is still exploratory. We are not treating any current prototype, interface, or hardware direction as final.

What is not announced

No final specification yet.

Zorix has not announced a final industrial design, display system, sensors, cameras, microphones, processor, operating system, battery life, model lineup, developer API, price, launch date, or regional availability for Zorix AI Glass.

Those details will only be published when the research is mature enough to support them.
