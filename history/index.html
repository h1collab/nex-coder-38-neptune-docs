Zorix evolution

It started with Bash.

Before Zorix became a broader AI platform, the story began on April 8, 2026 with a much simpler idea: make coding work better from the shell. That first stage was Nex Coder 1.5.

The beginning

April 8, 2026: Nex Coder 1.5 was Bash-first.

The earliest Zorix coding direction was centered around Bash and command-line work. Nex Coder 1.5 was not the broad platform Zorix is becoming today. It was a focused starting point: working from the terminal, reasoning about commands, and helping developers move through coding tasks from a shell-first environment.

That beginning matters because the core idea never disappeared. Zorix kept expanding the surface area, but the product direction stayed grounded in actually doing work rather than only producing isolated answers.

Evolution

From one shell-first coding model to a wider system.

2026-04-08

Bash and the command line.

Nex Coder 1.5 began on April 8, 2026. The first stage was narrow on purpose: coding through Bash, command-oriented reasoning, and a terminal-centered workflow.

This is the starting point of the Zorix evolution story.
2026-04-08

Star Image 1.5 launches.

On the same day Nex Coder 1.5 began, Zorix also released its first image-generation line: Star Image 1.5.

April 8, 2026 is the starting point for both the early coding line and the image-generation line.
2026-06

Star Image becomes Virexa.

In June 2026, the image line was renamed Virexa. At that stage there was no version number. The product was organized by effort levels rather than by numbered model generations.

2026-08

Virexa 3 merges the effort split.

Virexa 3 launched in August 2026 and consolidated the earlier effort-based split into one numbered generation.

Mid-2026-09

Virexa 3.5.

Virexa 3.5 arrived around the middle of September 2026, continuing the image model line after Virexa 3.

Expected 2026-10

Virexa 4 is next.

Zorix currently expects Virexa 4 to arrive in October 2026. The exact release date and final launch details have not been published here.

Nex Coder 2.x

Beyond individual shell commands.

The Nex Coder family expanded from the original Bash-first direction into broader coding workflows. Later 2.x releases moved Zorix toward larger development tasks and more complete software-building experiences.

Nex Coder 3.x

Repository-scale software engineering.

The 3.x generation pushed further into software engineering, long-running coding work, and more specialized model variants. The 3.8 generation ultimately included Mercury, Uranus, and Neptune.

2026-10-02

Nex Coder 4 Plutos.

Nex Coder 4 Plutos marked the next generation of the coding family. It was officially released on October 2, 2026, while access began through a staged rollout.

Today

Zorix is no longer only coding.

The platform now includes multiple directions: realtime conversation through Timeal, image generation through Virexa, speech-to-text through Transcribe, internal research through Thrym, and product experiences such as Zorix Code.
