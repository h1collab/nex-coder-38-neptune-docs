Story in motion.

The current v6 build turns story beats into in-game 3D scenes instead of leaving the campaign as a sequence of plain objective prompts.

Cinematics, radio, chapters.

Mid-gameThree additional 3D story cutscenes

The intro establishes the powered checkpoint, missing Patrol Twelve, armed security, the impossible Faceless frame, the relay objective, and the route toward extraction. Later cinematics trigger at the surveillance tower, ranger cabin, and the final relay sequence.

The current story structure includes chapters such as ENTER BLACKWOOD, ARMED RESPONSE, RANGER WOODS, DEAD SIGNAL, DEAD ZONE, and LAST SIGNAL.

Source basis: current v6 cinematic and audio work in h1collab/ggme, including the three in-game 3D story cutscene update.
