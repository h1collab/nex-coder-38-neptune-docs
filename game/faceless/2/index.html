Faceless 2.

Faceless 2 will have major differences from Faceless 1. It is being developed as a new generation, not simply a small content update to the first game.

Direction

Zorix has confirmed the direction at a high level: Faceless 2 is intended to differ significantly from Faceless 1. Final gameplay systems, world structure, enemies, story systems, release timing, supported platforms, and store availability have not been announced yet.
