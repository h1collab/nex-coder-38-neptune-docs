Generation 2 · Historical · Cancelled

Faceless 2.

Faceless 2 was explored as a major departure from Faceless 1, but development has ended. This page now preserves the prototype as historical context.

Historical development snapshot

The difference is already visible.

Before cancellation, Faceless 2 development had moved well beyond the structure of Faceless 1. The active v9 visual-QA development snapshot includes the Blackwood Incident setting, armed security encounters, pistol and rifle combat, evidence and relay objectives, voiced story beats, in-game 3D cinematics, a final counterattack, multiple game modes, mobile combat controls, and checkpoint continuation.

Archived snapshot only. These systems belonged to a cancelled prototype and are no longer being developed toward a Faceless 2 release.
