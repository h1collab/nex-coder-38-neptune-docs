Generation 2 · In development

Faceless 2.

Faceless 2 will have major differences from Faceless 1. It is being developed as a new generation, not simply a small content update to the first game.

Current development snapshot

The difference is already visible.

The current development work in the Faceless 2 project has moved well beyond the structure of Faceless 1. The active v6 development snapshot includes the Blackwood Incident setting, armed security encounters, pistol and rifle combat, evidence and relay objectives, voiced story beats, in-game 3D cinematics, a final counterattack, multiple game modes, mobile combat controls, and checkpoint continuation.

Development snapshot only. These systems are still in development and can change before release.
