v1.49.

The horror direction moved away from relying mainly on visible monster spawns and toward surveillance anomalies and things the player cannot directly observe.

What changed

The identity changes.

  • Removed the ventilation-duct creature death sequence.
  • Introduced missing frames and contradictory surveillance imagery.
  • Expanded directional sound, lighting failures, recordings, and experimental documents.
  • Expanded Project MIRROR and subject F-01.
  • Added procedural horror sound effects.
  • Added clearer SVG touch controls.
  • Updated to Godot 4.7 Stable / Forward Plus.
  • Announced as the last complete open-source edition.
