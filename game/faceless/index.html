FACELESS

Faceless is no longer an active Zorix game project. Development has ended, and the project is now preserved as part of Zorix Games history.

HistoricalDevelopment endedGeneration 1 · released June 26, 2026Faceless 2 · cancelled prototype
Status

Faceless is history now.

The first generation reached Google Play on June 26, 2026. Work later explored a second generation, including the Blackwood prototype and several experimental combat, cinematic, mobile, and systems directions.

That development will not continue as Faceless 2. The Faceless line has been closed and moved into the historical archive.

Current directionDevelopment in the ggme repository moved to School Brawl, a first-person classroom arcade combat / FPS direction.
Generation 1

The evolution that shipped.

Faceless began as a rough surveillance-horror beta and changed rapidly through community feedback. The early visible-monster structure evolved into missing frames, contradictory camera feeds, spatial audio, lighting failures, experimental records, and events that could not always be directly observed.
