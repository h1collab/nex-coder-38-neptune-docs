Overview

The Lost-and-Found Vault.

Level 22 is a dry, quiet storage vault filled with numbered shelves, lockers, rolling cages, and evidence bags. Many objects are ordinary; others seem personally connected to visitors.

Nothing in the vault should be assumed to belong to you simply because you recognize it.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Enter the vault door unlocked by a 0.00 scanner in Level 21.
  • Submit a blank claim ticket at Level 10 and follow the door marked FOUND.
Exits
  • Locker 23 can open into Level 23.
  • An evidence tag reading RETURN TO AISLE can send a visitor back to Level 0.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Some visitors find personal objects they lost years before entering NULVORIA.
  • Powered-off phones may contain photographs dated tomorrow.
  • A sealed bag can contain a handwritten description of the person currently opening it.
