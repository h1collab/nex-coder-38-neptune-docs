Overview

Receipt Rain.

Level 4 is composed of long rows of checkout lanes, darkened self-scan stations, and warm receipt printers that continuously spit out paper. The floor is often covered in curled receipts and shopping tokens.

Recovered image
Unsettling details

Details that do not fit.

  • Some receipts print conversations seconds before they happen.
  • Nearly every total ends in .17, regardless of the items listed.
  • The final line often reads: THANK YOU FOR WAITING AT 3:17.
Archive caution: repeated observations are consistent enough to record, but NULVORIA does not always reproduce the same behavior for every traveler.
Exits

Recorded transitions.

  • A warm receipt printed with a map may guide you back to Level 0.
  • Following the last open lane can lead to stranger retail zones.
