Overview

The Intercom Chapel.

Level 18 resembles a department-store atrium stripped of merchandise and filled with public-address speakers. Mannequins occupy display plinths in apparently deliberate arrangements.

The phrase THANK YOU FOR LISTENING AT 03:17 appears at the end of every complete recording.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Enter the potting shed in Level 17 when music can be heard inside.
  • Open the security shutter from Level 15 after all monitors go dark.
Exits
  • A booth-like door can lead back to Level 9.
  • A cracked service door can reopen into Level 11.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Announcements sometimes answer thoughts that were never spoken aloud.
  • Mannequins have been recorded changing posture between consecutive blinks.
  • Long broadcasts contain sequences of product numbers that match prices from other levels.
Archive caution: repeated reports are recorded as patterns, not guarantees. NULVORIA may reproduce the same location differently for different visitors.
