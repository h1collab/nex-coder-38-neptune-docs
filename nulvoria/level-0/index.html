The Endless Mart

A convenience store of apparently infinite size. Every clock, register display, microwave timer, and wall clock that still works reads exactly 3:17 AM.

Description

The store does not end.

Level 0 resembles a late-night convenience store built from repeating aisles, drink refrigerators, cashier islands, microwave stations, cleaning closets, and stock corridors. Walking in a straight line does not reliably return to the same location, and identical aisles may contain different stock when revisited.

The front doors show a dark parking lot, but no confirmed record describes a normal route through them.

Archive note: clocks remain fixed at 03:17, while ordinary needs such as sleep, food, batteries, and rest still change normally.

Changing aisles

Entity

The Night Clerk.

The Night Clerk appears behind certain checkout counters wearing an ordinary convenience-store uniform. It is considered neutral and can trade supplies, batteries, maps, and other useful objects.

Its speech is brief and repetitive. Directions given by the Clerk are often useful, although they may refer to aisle numbers or stockrooms that were not visible before the conversation.

Exits

Two major transitions are recorded.

  • Storeroom → Level 2. Certain stockrooms continue into a loading corridor. Level 2 is not documented yet.
  • Cold room → Level 3. Some walk-in refrigerators extend farther than the building should allow. Level 3 is not documented yet.

Checkout and staff areas may also connect to Level 1.
