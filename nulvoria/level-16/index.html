Overview

The Detergent Sea.

Level 16 is partially flooded by a white-blue detergent mixture. The depth varies without warning, and buoyant product displays drift through aisles that appear to continue beneath the surface.

No archive team has measured a consistent bottom depth.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Follow a major spill corridor in Level 5.
  • Open the steel container door in Level 12 when foam is visible beneath it.
Exits
  • An emergency ladder can climb to Level 17.
  • A drain hatch occasionally drops into the cold corridors of Level 3.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Large soap bubbles can reflect memories instead of the viewer's face.
  • The foam level has been observed rising after a visitor says their own name aloud.
  • Loose price tags drift against the visible current as if pulled toward an unseen point.
Archive caution: repeated reports are recorded as patterns, not guarantees. NULVORIA may reproduce the same location differently for different visitors.
