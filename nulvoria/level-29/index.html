Overview

The missing page is a place.

Level 29 resembles fragments of every earlier NULVORIA record after the structure has been stripped of context. A checkout counter may stand alone in a concrete corridor. A freezer door may open into an employee bedroom. A yellow elevator may have no wall around it.

Text appears where architecture should be: 404, RETURN, NULL, ALREADY_ENTERED. These are not painted signs. They occupy physical space and can sometimes be walked around.

Archive contradiction: the first references to Level 29 were recovered from Level 28 before this record existed. Several copies of those receipts now contain a timestamp matching the first time this page was opened.
Entrances

How Level 29 is reached.

  • Use the black service door behind the register in Level 28 after receiving a receipt that reads LEVEL 29 — ALREADY ENTERED.
  • Follow a corridor whose directional sign becomes a literal broken URL-like string.
  • Rarely, opening a door that should lead to a deleted, sealed, or nonexistent room produces Level 29 instead.
No entrance has been reproduced on command. Most arrivals occur immediately after the visitor concludes that a route is impossible.
Exits

404 does not always mean trapped.

  • A white doorway marked BACK may return the visitor to Level 28.
  • A checkout receipt reading FOUND has returned two documented visitors to Level 0.
  • A black rectangle labeled /home has been observed three times. No visitor who entered it has returned to the archive, so its destination is unknown.

Archive note

The first impossible timestamp.

The oldest known Level 29 artifact predates its own recovery record by four days. The metadata is internally consistent. If the timestamp is genuine, NULVORIA recorded the archive reading the artifact before the archive possessed it.

Do not treat a 404 inside NULVORIA as proof that nothing is there.
