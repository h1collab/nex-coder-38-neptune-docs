Overview

The Furniture Showrooms.

Level 23 is built from living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, and staged apartments with no exterior walls. Lamps work, beds are made, and many televisions show silent home-video footage.

Archive policy forbids sleeping in a room whose family photographs change after lights-out.
