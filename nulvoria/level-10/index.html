Overview

The Return Counter.

Level 10 is a customer-service floor where desks repeat in long rows and the queue system never reaches zero. Most counters are empty, but desk lamps, ticket dispensers, and thermal printers remain powered.

One archive form requests the return of 'time spent outside' and leaves the quantity field blank.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Ring a customer-service bell in Level 4 three times while no receipt printer is active.
  • Use a lost-ticket kiosk in Level 8 when it displays RETURN.
Exits
  • An EMPLOYEES ONLY corridor can lead to Level 15.
  • A rolling service shutter marked R-12 opens into Level 12.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Return forms often describe objects the visitor has never owned.
  • The phrase NEXT CUSTOMER is sometimes spoken in the visitor's own voice.
  • Receipt timestamps occasionally count backward toward 03:17 instead of forward.
