The Garden Center at Dawn

An indoor greenhouse and garden center under a false dawn sky that never becomes morning.

Overview

The Garden Center at Dawn.

Level 17 contains greenhouse aisles, wet stone paths, plant tables, fertilizer shelves, and an artificial horizon glowing with permanent early-morning light.

The Groundskeeper trades tools and water but refuses to answer direct questions about the sky.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Open a greenhouse door that occasionally appears in Level 0.
  • Climb the chained stair from Level 14.
Exits
  • A potting shed can open into Level 18.
  • A fog-covered side gate may return to Level 6.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Plants consistently turn toward footsteps rather than toward the brightest light.
  • Some seed packets contain names of flowers that do not exist in any known catalog.
  • The false dawn never becomes morning, even after days of uninterrupted observation.
