The Light We Left On

A small break room hidden somewhere inside the service corridors of Level 1. It is quiet, warm, finite, and remembered less for what lives there than for the people who once waited there together.

Description

A room that should not be enough.

Level 1.12 is a single employee break room measuring approximately seven metres by five. There are two chairs, one table, a sink, three lockers, a vending machine that rarely takes anything in return, and a small amber lamp beside the door.

Unlike most of NULVORIA, the room does not expand when measured. Food does not spoil quickly. The fluorescent hum of Level 1 cannot be heard through the walls. Visitors consistently describe the same unusual feeling: for a little while, they do not feel watched.

The lamp has never been observed switching off by itself.
Recovered story

Mira and Theo.

The archive knows their names only because they wrote them on the underside of the table.

Mira arrived first. Her earliest note says she had been walking Level 1 for almost two days and had started marking doors because the corridors kept repeating. Theo arrived later carrying a half-empty bottle of water and a broken flashlight. Neither note explains how he found the room.

They stayed.

Not forever. Just long enough to turn the room into something that felt like a place rather than a trap.

They numbered the cans in the cupboard. They fixed one chair with tape. They argued about whether the vending machine coffee counted as coffee. On the wall, Theo drew a tiny window with a sunrise outside it. Mira added two birds because, according to her note, “one looked lonely.”

Weeks later, Mira became sick. There was medicine in other levels, but no route from 1.12 stayed open for long. Theo began leaving each morning to search Level 1 and returning before the amber lamp dimmed.

One night he returned and the room was empty.

Her blanket was folded. Her cup was washed. The chair had been pushed under the table.

On the wall beneath the painted sunrise was a final note in Mira's handwriting.

You kept the light on long enough.
Don't stay because I might come back.
Leave it on for the next person.

The archive cannot verify where Mira went. Theo stayed for three more nights. On the fourth, the digital clock changed.

03:17 → 03:18

It remained at 03:18 for exactly sixty seconds.

During that minute, the break-room door opened directly onto Level 0.

Theo left the lamp on.

What remains

The room remembers kindness better than people.

Later visitors have found new supplies placed neatly beside the two chairs. No one has seen who leaves them. The sunrise drawing remains on the wall, although the plaster around it has been replaced more than once.

The two birds are always there.

Visitors are encouraged to leave one useful item if they can spare it. The archive does not require this. People simply keep doing it.

The current note beside the lamp reads: REST. DRINK SOME WATER. YOU DO NOT HAVE TO SOLVE EVERYTHING TONIGHT.
Entrances

Finding 1.12.

  • In Level 1, follow an employee corridor until a plain door appears labeled BREAK ROOM 1.12.
  • The door is most often reported by exhausted, injured, or severely lost visitors.
  • Searching specifically for Level 1.12 appears to make the door less likely to appear.
Exits

Leaving without losing it.

  • The same door normally returns to Level 1.
  • Rarely, after a visitor has slept, eaten, or left a note for someone else, the clock changes to 03:18 for one minute. During that minute the door has opened to Level 0.
  • No confirmed exit leads outside NULVORIA.
Do not wait indefinitely for 03:18. The room is safe, but it is not home.
