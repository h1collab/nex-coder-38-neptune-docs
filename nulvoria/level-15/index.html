Overview

The Monitor Nest.

Level 15 contains dense security rooms connected by cable-filled corridors. Screens show many known levels, but some feeds display places not yet visited or rooms that do not exist in the current archive.

Archive operators stopped numbering cameras after CAMERA 0317 began appearing on systems with fewer than 100 channels.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Follow the staff corridor from Level 10 until the ceiling fills with camera cabling.
  • Take the cable lift down from Level 11.
Exits
  • A security shutter can open into Level 18.
  • Maintenance stairs marked STORE LEVEL can return to Level 1.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • One monitor may show the visitor from behind even when the room is empty.
  • Unplugged screens sometimes remain lit and continue updating.
  • Some camera feeds show a room several minutes before the visitor physically arrives there.
