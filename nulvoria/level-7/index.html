Overview

The Toy Promotion Hall.

Level 7 resembles a promotional retail hall assembled from toy displays, cardboard standees, sale banners, and low-lit prize shelves. The space feels brightly decorated at first, but its aisles become unnervingly quiet between bursts of music.
