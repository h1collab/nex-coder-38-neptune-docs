Overview

The Employee Cafeteria.

Level 24 resembles a large institutional cafeteria connected to staff kitchens and dishwashing tunnels. Trays appear on serving rails without observable preparation.

The safest food is sealed. Warm prepared meals have not produced consistent effects.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Use the service door behind a kitchen display in Level 23.
  • Open the kitchen hatch in Level 13 when a meal ticket slides out.
Exits
  • The dishwashing tunnel can descend to Level 25.
  • A rear staff door can return to Level 1.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Meal receipts may list the exact food a visitor was thinking about before arriving.
  • Empty tables occasionally produce overlapping conversations from unseen diners.
  • A tray left unattended can return with one bite missing despite no one approaching it.
