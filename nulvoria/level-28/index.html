Overview

The Last Checkout.

Level 28 is unusually simple compared with earlier levels. One checkout lane remains powered. A clerk-shaped figure may stand behind it wearing a blank name badge. The surrounding store is dark and has no confirmed perimeter.

Level 29 is not currently documented. The archive has no verified explanation for the printed reference.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Open the SUNRISE door in Level 27.
  • Follow the final illuminated lane in Level 4 when every other register goes dark.
Exits
  • A front entrance may return to Level 0 when the receipt prints PAID.
  • A black service door behind the register has an unknown destination and is not considered a confirmed exit.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Some receipts include one additional line: LEVEL 29 — ALREADY ENTERED.
  • The Final Clerk occasionally asks whether the visitor wants a receipt before any item has been placed on the counter.
