Overview

The Lost Queue.

Level 8 resembles a giant queuing zone made from metal lane barriers, scanner pedestals, and forgotten shopping baskets. The lanes weave and reconnect in ways that make direct movement difficult, even when the space appears open.

Unsettling details

Details that do not fit.

  • Basket contents may rearrange to match what you were thinking about.
  • Queue signs sometimes show your initials instead of a lane number.
  • The farther lanes seem to advance even when nobody moves.
Archive caution: repeated observations are consistent enough to record, but NULVORIA does not always reproduce the same behavior for every traveler.
