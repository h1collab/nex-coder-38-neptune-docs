Overview

The Forecourt.

Level 6 resembles an empty service station at night: fuel pumps, concrete lanes, a flickering kiosk sign, and wet asphalt reflecting pale light. The horizon remains dark and strangely distant no matter how far you walk.

Unsettling details

Details that do not fit.

  • Parked cars may have warm engines but completely empty seats.
  • Pump displays sometimes count upward without dispensing fuel.
  • The asphalt occasionally reflects a sunrise that never appears overhead.
Archive caution: repeated observations are consistent enough to record, but NULVORIA does not always reproduce the same behavior for every traveler.
