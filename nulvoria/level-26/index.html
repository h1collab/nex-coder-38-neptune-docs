Overview

The Yellow Elevator.

Level 26 is not a conventional floor. It is a network of nearly identical yellow elevator cars and narrow service lobbies connected by doors that open to different levels without warning.

Holding the doors open does not prevent the car from moving.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Use the yellow elevator in Level 25.
  • Enter a freight elevator in Level 12 when its panel contains more than twenty floor buttons.
Exits
  • Button 27 frequently opens into Level 27.
  • Several numbered buttons return to earlier levels, but destinations can change between rides.
Special route

89.2.

After some safe return trips, an extra button marked 89.2 may appear. It leads to Level 89.2 — The Lift Commons, a formerly lethal elevator lobby now maintained as a staffed transit refuge.

Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • A new button may appear labeled with the visitor's name instead of a floor number.
  • The floor indicator sometimes displays negative numbers or symbols not used elsewhere in the archive.
  • Mirrors can show another passenger even when the elevator is physically empty.
