Overview

The Vending Aisle.

Level 5 resembles a narrow retail passage lined with vending machines and illuminated glass-front coolers. The hum of compressors and ad screens fills the air, and the aisle seems longer each time it is crossed.

Unsettling details

Details that do not fit.

  • One item label in each row may appear in your own handwriting.
  • Reflections in cooler glass sometimes blink after you do.
  • Screens occasionally display a perfect live view of the empty aisle directly behind you.
Archive caution: repeated observations are consistent enough to record, but NULVORIA does not always reproduce the same behavior for every traveler.
Exits

Recorded transitions.
