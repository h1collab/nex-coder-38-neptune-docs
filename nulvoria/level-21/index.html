Overview

The Price-Check Corridor.

Level 21 consists of white retail walls fitted with hundreds of self-service barcode scanners. Most screens are blank until something passes beneath them.

Do not repeatedly scan the same personal object; later results tend to become more specific.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Stop the yellow-marked escalator in Level 20.
  • Scan a receipt at an inactive price checker in Level 4.
Exits
  • A scanner displaying 0.00 unlocks the vault door to Level 22.
  • A mirrored staff door can return to Level 9.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Scanning a hand may display a full legal name and an impossible product category.
  • Some screens show dates instead of prices, including dates later than the current archive record.
  • A scanner may beep before an object is placed beneath it.
