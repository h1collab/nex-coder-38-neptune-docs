Overview

The Employees' Village.

Level 19 resembles a small residential district built entirely from employee break rooms, dormitories, kitchens, and display apartments. Every unit looks recently occupied, but no confirmed resident behaves like a normal person.

Several doors display employee names that match people reported missing outside the archive.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Follow a break-room corridor in Level 1 past the third identical vending machine.
  • Use a locker room door in Level 15 labeled RESIDENT ACCESS.
Exits
  • A concrete stair marked COMMUTE leads to Level 20.
  • A red fire door can return to Level 10.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Framed staff photographs sometimes include the visitor before they entered NULVORIA.
  • Apartment calendars show different months but all contain handwritten notes about the visitor's future routine.
  • Some refrigerators contain food labeled with a date corresponding to the visitor's birthday.
