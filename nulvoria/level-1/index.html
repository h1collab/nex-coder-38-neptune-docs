Description

Behind the storefront.

Level 1 contains spaces that resemble the staff-only side of a very large convenience store. The architecture repeats in long sections: cashier counters, employee corridors, refrigerator banks, stock rooms, utility spaces, and loading passages.

The lighting is weaker than Level 0, and the constant sound of refrigeration can make distant areas feel closer than they are.

Areas

Repeated service zones.

Zone A

Checkout islands

Rows of powered registers and counters that repeat with small differences.

Zone B

Employee halls

Narrow corridors connecting staff rooms, utility spaces, and storage doors.

Zone C

Cold aisles

Long refrigerator banks with constant compressor noise and occasional restocked shelves.

Zone D

Loading passages

Concrete service routes containing stock carts, sealed doors, and occasional returns to familiar areas.

Entity

The Night Clerk can appear here too.

Night Clerk sightings continue in Level 1, usually near powered registers or stock carts. As in Level 0, the Clerk is considered neutral and can trade useful supplies.

Navigation

Returning to Level 0.

Some staff doors reconnect to Level 0. Some deeper routes remain inconsistent, but the archive now documents Levels 2–8.
