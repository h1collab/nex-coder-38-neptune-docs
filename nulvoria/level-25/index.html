Overview

The Lost Parking Deck.

Level 25 is a concrete parking deck with low ceilings, fluorescent strips, and rows of mostly ordinary vehicles. No outdoor edge reveals a recognizable city or landscape.

No vehicle has successfully reached an exit ramp.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

  • Use the rear staff door from Level 24 when it opens onto concrete instead of a corridor.

Exits

Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Some cars are unlocked and have warm engines despite being empty.
  • Car radios occasionally whisper nearby license-plate numbers before their headlights turn on.
  • Parking-space numbers can change while the visitor is standing inside the marked space.
