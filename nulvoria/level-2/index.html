Overview

The Storeroom Maze.

Level 2 resembles the back-of-house storage zone of an impossibly large store. Metal shelving, shrink-wrapped pallets, plastic curtains, and beige service doors repeat in patterns that do not match any stable floor plan.

Unsettling details

Details that do not fit.

  • Some boxes are labeled with the names of people who have never entered NULVORIA.
  • Open cartons sometimes release warm air and a distant checkout beep, even when no register is nearby.
  • Inventory tags occasionally list tomorrow’s date.
Archive caution: repeated observations are consistent enough to record, but NULVORIA does not always reproduce the same behavior for every traveler.
