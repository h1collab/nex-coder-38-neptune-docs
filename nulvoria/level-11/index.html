Overview

The Broadcast Ceiling.

Level 11 exists above suspended ceilings and lighting grids. Narrow catwalks pass over dark retail voids while public-address speakers continue broadcasting staff messages from unknown sources.

Do not follow an announcement that uses your full name followed by 'please remain where you are'.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Climb a ceiling hatch in Level 1 that opens above an active speaker line.
  • Use a ladder concealed behind a promotion wall in Level 7.
Exits
  • A cable lift can descend to Level 15.
  • A broken access ladder sometimes drops into Level 5.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • The PA system may announce a visitor's next movement before it happens.
  • Safety signs can change wording immediately after being read.
  • Some shadows appear attached to the ceiling rather than to the person casting them.
