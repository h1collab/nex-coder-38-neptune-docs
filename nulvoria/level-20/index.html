Overview

The Endless Escalator.

Level 20 contains banks of escalators crossing a dark multi-level void. Some run upward, some downward, and some move sideways despite being built normally.

Walking against an escalator does not reliably change your vertical position.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Take the COMMUTE stair from Level 19.
  • Enter a mall-like corridor from Level 8 and follow the sound of escalator motors.
Exits
  • Press an emergency stop on a yellow-marked escalator to access Level 21.
  • A side door beside an inactive landing can return to Level 13.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Reflections in the metal side panels sometimes continue riding after the visitor steps off.
  • Floor indicators repeat 3, 1, 7 in different orders.
  • A person visible on the opposite escalator may remain the same distance away for hours.
