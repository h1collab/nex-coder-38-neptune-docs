Overview

The Wet Loading Bay.

Level 12 resembles the loading infrastructure behind a very large retail complex. Shallow water covers much of the floor, and rows of shutter doors open onto loading spaces that do not share consistent geometry.

A recurring trailer bears the number 0317 but has never been opened successfully.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Follow a pallet tunnel from Level 2 until the shelving changes to concrete walls.
  • Pass beneath the service shutter marked R-12 in Level 10.
Exits
  • A drainage stair can emerge near the pumps of Level 6.
  • A steel shipping-container door can open into Level 16.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Standing water sometimes ripples several seconds before footsteps arrive.
  • Trailer numbers disappear when counted twice in the same order.
  • Engines can idle for hours inside trucks with no drivers, keys, or fuel.
