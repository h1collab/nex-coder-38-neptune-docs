NULVORIA

Twenty-nine documented environments connected by unstable retail, service, industrial, domestic, and impossible routes. Every record now includes a survival class; Level 9 onward also documents explicit entrances and exits.

NULVORIA is fictional worldbuilding. “Survivor” reports, entities, photographs and recovered records are in-universe material.
Recurring pattern

03:17 keeps returning.

The number appears on clocks, receipts, camera channels, queue displays, trailers and unrelated systems. Level 27 introduces a second fixed time — 05:59 — yet its receipts still print 03:17.

Level 28 contains the first unverified reference to LEVEL 29 — ALREADY ENTERED. No public Level 29 record exists.
