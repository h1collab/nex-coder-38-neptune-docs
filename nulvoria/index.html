NULVORIA

Ordinary spaces that continue beyond where architecture should end. The archive now contains Level 0 through Level 8, including survival classes, resources, entities, routes, and recovered in-universe field imagery.

NULVORIA is fictional worldbuilding. “Survivor photographs,” archive reports, entities, and recovered records are in-universe material.
Survival classes

Class 0–5.

The class summarizes overall survival difficulty, not a guarantee. A lower class can still contain isolated anomalies, and a familiar route may change between visits.

CLASS 0

Stable

No confirmed entity pressure; predictable resources and routes.

CLASS 1

Low risk

Resources are available and navigation is generally manageable.

CLASS 2

Moderate

Noticeable anomalies or route instability; preparation recommended.

CLASS 3

Significant

Environmental or entity hazards can interfere with survival.

CLASS 4

Severe

High anomaly activity and limited safe movement.

CLASS 5

Extreme

Prolonged stays are strongly discouraged; conditions may change without warning.

Archive note

The numbers do not describe distance.

A higher Level number does not mean the location is physically deeper. Routes can skip levels, loop backward, or return to an earlier area through a door that did not exist before.

Repeated number: 03:17. It appears on clocks, receipts, queue displays, and unrelated systems across multiple documented Levels. The archive does not currently have an explanation.
