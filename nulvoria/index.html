NULVORIA

Thirty numbered Levels are now documented, from Level 0 through Level 29, plus the special sublevels Level 1.12 and Level 3.141. The archive includes survival classes, unstable routes, entities, recovered records, and spaces that do not behave like normal architecture.

NULVORIA is fictional worldbuilding. “Survivor” reports, entities, photographs and recovered records are in-universe material.
Recurring pattern

03:17 keeps returning.

The number appears on clocks, receipts, camera channels, queue displays, trailers and unrelated systems. Level 27 introduces a second fixed time — 05:59 — yet its receipts still print 03:17.

Level 28 contained the first reference to LEVEL 29 — ALREADY ENTERED. That reference now resolves to a public record—but Level 29 claims the visitor opened it before the record existed.
