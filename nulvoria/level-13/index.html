Overview

The Bakery After Close.

Level 13 is warmer than most documented levels. Commercial ovens remain hot despite disconnected gas lines, proofing racks move between rooms, and the smell of fresh bread can be detected from corridors that contain no bakery equipment.

The messages are usually short. The most repeated one is YOU WERE HERE BEFORE.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Open a heated service door behind the food aisle of Level 0.
  • Pass through a deli curtain in Level 1 when the room behind it is lit amber.
Exits
  • A flour storeroom can return to Level 2.
  • A cooling-room hatch marked 14 leads to Level 14.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Fresh loaves sometimes rise with handprint-shaped marks inside the crust.
  • Every functioning oven timer eventually rings at exactly 03:17.
  • Icing on unfinished cakes may briefly form readable messages before melting flat.
