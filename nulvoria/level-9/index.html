Overview

The Pharmacy Rows.

Level 9 resembles an oversized pharmacy embedded inside NULVORIA. White shelving repeats in clean rows, consultation booths are usually locked, and medicine cabinets continue behind walls that should lead outside the building.

Several recovered labels list allergies never disclosed to anyone inside NULVORIA.
Routes

Entrances & exits.

Entrances
  • Follow a PHARMACY sign that appears above an unused aisle in Level 4.
  • Open a medicine shelf in Level 0 that has a door hinge where the back panel should be.
Exits
  • A vaccine cooler can open into Level 3.
  • A consultation booth whose ticket display reads 10 leads to Level 10.
Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Prescription labels sometimes carry the visitor's full name and a date that has not happened yet.
  • A cough may echo from an aisle that remains completely empty when checked.
  • Shelf tags can rearrange overnight into short warnings such as DO NOT ASK FOR THE NIGHT SHIFT.
