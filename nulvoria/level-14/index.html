Overview

The Cart Graveyard.

Level 14 resembles the outer cart-storage area of a hypermarket scaled far beyond normal proportions. Rows of nested carts create corridors between chain-link fences, service sheds, and painted parking lines.

The Pusher is rarely seen directly; the sound of one damaged wheel usually arrives first.

Unsettling details

What does not fit.

  • Shopping carts frequently rearrange themselves into circles while no one is watching.
  • Coins left in cart handles are often warm to the touch even in cold weather.
  • Wheel squeaks occasionally resemble incomplete spoken sentences when heard from far away.
